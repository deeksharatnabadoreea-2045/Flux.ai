When the orchestrator asks you to improve yourself, change architecture, or hillclimb:

1. Name one failure mode and one success metric.
2. Propose **one lever** (instructions, tool, eval, routing, or architecture).
3. Call `propose_evolution` with a complete patch and rollback plan.
4. Do **not** assume the change is live until they accept on the company floor.

After accept, read `evolution-appendix.md` in this agent folder if it exists.
