# Product — scientist co-pilot

## What customers get today

A **chatbot** that acts like a lab partner:

- Ask in plain language
- **Desk agents** (Scout, Maker, Ledger, …) do the work and **file** results
- Human lead reviews accept / return / kill (optional self-serve later)

## What you charge for

Paid **agent** access—not simulation supercomputers yet:

- Per report, per seat, or monthly bundle of briefs
- 24/7 watch on linked sites (e.g. ClinicalTrials.gov)

## What we build underneath

**In-silico human trials** (see [PLATFORM-NORTH-STAR.md](./PLATFORM-NORTH-STAR.md)): when the replay harness works, upsell back-tests and pilots—not a new product, a deeper tier.

## Flux HQ vs chat

| Surface | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **Chatbot** (product) | Where scientists talk |
| **HQ floor** (internal / power users) | Orchestrator console, linked sources, evolution accept |

Ship the chat first; HQ remains the ops backbone.
